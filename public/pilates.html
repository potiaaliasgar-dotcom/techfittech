Where can I buy STOTT PILATES equipment in India?

TechFit is an authorised reseller of Merrithew STOTT PILATES equipment in India, supplying Reformers, Cadillac trapeze tables, Split Pedal Stability Chairs, Ladder Barrels, Spine Correctors, Stability Barres and props, with delivery, installation and after sales support across India.

What is the difference between Merrithew STOTT PILATES and TechFit Pilates?

Merrithew STOTT PILATES is the Canadian gold standard in Pilates apparatus, CE marked with limited lifetime warranties. TechFit Pilates is our own label studio range in Oak, White Maple and aluminium, offering the full apparatus line at exceptional value. TechFit supplies and installs both.

How much does a Pilates Reformer cost in India?

Reformer pricing depends on the model, frame material, spring system and whether a tower is included. TechFit quotes per configuration across both ranges. Contact us on WhatsApp or call +91 98201 66910 for a quote.

Do you set up complete Pilates studios?

Yes. TechFit designs the studio layout, selects the equipment mix, supplies and installs it pan India, and can arrange instructor training and after sales servicing including replacement springs, ropes and parts.

Which Pilates Reformer is best for a home studio?

For home use we recommend the Merrithew At Home SPX Reformer Package or the TechFit Pilates foldable Reformers in Oak or aluminium, which fold compact for storage without compromising the workout.
