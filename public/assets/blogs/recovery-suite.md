When a leading wellness club in Mumbai approached TechFit in late 2025 to design and execute a commercial longevity suite, their goal was clear: integrate clinical grade Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT), whole body electric cryotherapy, and commercial contrast ice baths into a seamless 2,500 square foot facility without fragmented contractor management.

Here is an inside look at the spatial planning, MEP electrical engineering, equipment selection, and execution timeline behind one of India's premier commercial recovery installations.

## The Brief and Space Zoning

The project required converting a raw commercial floor plate into four dedicated recovery zones:

1. **Hyperbaric Oxygen Chamber Suite (300 sq ft):** Acoustic insulation, dedicated 16A electrical line, and continuous ambient climate control maintaining 22 degrees Celsius.
2. **Electric Cryotherapy Chamber Cabin (400 sq ft):** 3-phase commercial electrical supply, sub-floor load balancing, and external condenser heat dissipation ducting.
3. **Contrast Therapy Room (600 sq ft):** Dual commercial cold plunge tubs paired with a 4-person Canadian Hemlock full spectrum infrared sauna, non-slip drainage flooring, and high-volume humidity exhaust.
4. **Neuro Recovery and Longevity Lounge (1,200 sq ft):** Red light photobiomodulation beds, dry floatation pod, and PEMF multi-therapy loungers for passive recovery.

## Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing (MEP) Execution

Integrating clinical grade wellness equipment into commercial real estate requires rigorous engineering:

* **Electrical Capacity:** Total connected electrical load of 38 kW across 3-phase 415V and single-phase 220V circuits with dedicated surge protection and automated phase changers.
* **Thermal Management:** Electric cryotherapy compressors reject significant heat. TechFit engineered specialized acoustic exterior ducting routing condenser exhaust safely to the building perimeter.
* **Plumbing and Drainage:** High-traffic cold plunges require rapid drainage access and commercial water supply lines with preliminary dual-stage particulate filtration.
